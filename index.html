.DISCUSSION...
Issued at 242 AM CDT Wed Oct 21 2026

Early this morning, a upper level shortwave is rounding the upper 
level ridge over the Plains. This shortwave is moving into the local 
area providing enough lift to produce weak returns on radar moving 
into the eastern Kansas and western Missouri. These returns are 
falling into dry air below 10kft which is keeping any precipitation 
relegated to brief very light sprinkles. These brief sprinkles may 
continue until mid-morning when the upper shortwave will weaken and 
surface high pressure builds into the area from the east. Despite 
surface high pressure providing weak mixing today, height rises 
across the area due to the upper level ridge becoming more amplified 
will drive highs into the low to mid 70s. Continued height rises are 
expected Thursday as the upper level ridge axis moves from the 
central Plains to the eastern Plains in response to a upper level 
trough moving into the California coast. This will drive highs into 
the mid to upper 70s. Friday and Saturday, the upper level ridge 
axis moves directly over the area providing further height rises 
coupled with modest WAA. This will aid in highs rising into the mid 
70 to near 80. 

By Sunday, the aforementioned upper level trough will move from the 
Great Basin to the Eastern Rockies weakening as it does. This will 
force an attendant cold front into the Plains on Sunday. The local 
area will remain in the warm sector ahead of the front with highs 
again in the 70s. Monday, weak trough will move into the central 
Plains finally forcing the cold front into the CWA providing the 
next chance for precipitation. The upper trough will then move 
through the region on Monday night continuing shower chances as the 
front is forcing through the CWA. Temperatures in Monday will range 
from the lower 60s to lower 70s. Surface high pressure will build 
into the area behind the cold front cooling highs to near seasonal 
normals in the upper 50s to mid 60s.
