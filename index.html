.DISCUSSION...
Issued at 218 PM CDT Mon Oct 19 2026

The prevailing pattern over the middle of the country for the next 
week will be characterized by varying magnitudes/placement of 
upper-level ridging. Initially, that ridging is somewhat 
suppressed with the ridge axis shifted east of the area. This is
allowing a weak upper- level shortwave to spread cloud cover 
over the area as that wave tracks to the east through the 
region. There have been some returns show up on regional 
composite reflectivity but with a deep layer of dry air through 
at least the lowest 12000 ft of the atmosphere, there is only a 
small chance for anything hitting the ground. A similar scenario
will play out tomorrow with the ridge suppressed to the 
southeast of the area. A weak upper-level shortwave trough will 
move across the Central Plains, leading to mainly just 
increasing cloud cover. With a slightly better moisture profile 
though, can't rule out a brief shower or sprinkle, mainly over 
eastern KS and adjacent areas of Missouri. Still, PoPs are 
10-15% or less through the area. 

By the middle of the week, upper-level ridging strengthens through 
the Plains states, leading to well above normal temperatures across 
the area and suppressing any chance for precipitation. That persists 
through the end of the week before the ridge axis again shifts to 
the east and then flattens out by early next week. As this occurs, 
it will allow for low chances for precipitation to return to the 
area. But even this looks confined to far northwestern Missouri and 
extreme northeastern Kansas. The rest of the area is still very 
likely (>90%) to stay dry through the entire forecast.

the region, will decrease overnight. Though another disturbance
tomorrow afternoon will increase mid and high level clouds.
