.DISCUSSION...
Issued at 156 PM CDT Fri Oct 16 2026

With the advection of cooler air in the wake of yesterdays cold 
front, temperatures throughout the rest of the day today will be in 
the upper 40s and low 50s. Synoptically, a 500mb positively tilted 
trough is digging over our region, with strong winds aloft as the 
jet stream passes right overhead. This has allowed for a large 
pressure gradient in response to high pressure over the western 
Great Plains, and the low pressure system that exited to our east 
yesterday. As a result, expect gusty winds out of the north up to 
30mph, before diminishing in the evening hours as we lose the 
daytime mixing. Throughout the evening and overnight hours, the 
surface high to our west, along with dry air to our north will both 
approach our area. This will help push off any remaining cloud 
cover, and allow temperatures to drop into the 30s. Portions of 
areas north of Interstate 70 will likely dip down into the low 30s, 
prompting the issuance of a Freeze Warning to our northern counties. 
The rest of the area will get down into the mid-30s, with many of 
those areas having a Frost Advisory in effect. Precaution will 
include protecting any sensitive plants, crops, and vegetation.

Saturday and Sunday will be quiet as our trough moves off to the 
east, along with the high pressure approaching. We will also see 
little if any cloud cover this weekend, as drier air advects from 
our north. Temperatures will also climb back up into the 60s, 
especially on Sunday as pressure heights increase and warm air 
advection moves into our region. Conditions will remain quiet 
throughout next week, with the ridge staying in place, and allowing 
southerly flow to advect warm air. Temperatures then will return 
into the 70s and 80s by the middle part of the week. While this 
happens, a trough will begin to develop off the west coast, and will 
strengthen as it interacts with a subtropical jet. As this 
disturbance gets its act together, it will move into our area by 
late next week and into the weekend. Exact timing and effects still 
remain to be seen, as certainty will increase as we get closer to 
the time period.
