.DISCUSSION...
Issued at 232 AM CDT Tue Oct 20 2026

Today, the local area will be under low-amplitude upper level 
ridging. A subtle shortwave will move through the area this 
afternoon. This feature may provide enough lift to produce a few 
sprinkles however, dry area below 10kft will most likely relegate 
precipitation (10-15%) to virga. This feature will provide mostly 
cloudy to cloudy skies today however, the low amplitude ridging and 
weak WAA will still allow highs to rise into the lower 70s. Another 
stronger shortwave rounding the upper ridge will move through the 
central Plains tonight and approach the local area. However, models 
suggest this feature weakens as it approaches and dry air below 10kft 
continues to be in place and may actually be further reinforced by a 
surface high pressure ridge building back westward from the Ohio and 
mid-Mississippi River Valleys. Consequently, precipitation chances 
remain 10-15% tonight over the eastern Kansas and western Missouri. 
Tomorrow, surface high pressure continues to build back into the 
area as upper level ridging become more amplified over the central 
CONUS in response to a upper level trough digging south along the 
west coast. Despite weak mixing, height rises will aid in highs 
rising into the low to mid 70s. Thursday will present similar 
conditions to Wednesday with continued height rises as upper level 
ridging builds over the central CONUS. This will push highs into the 
mid to upper 70s.

The gradual warming trend through midweek continues Friday and 
Saturday as the upper level ridge axis moves directly over the area. 
This will provide continued height rises pushing highs into the mid 
70s to lower 80s by Saturday. Saturday into Saturday night a upper 
level trough will across the Canadian Plains forcing a sagging cold 
front towards the area. This front is not expected to reach the area 
Saturday night but a few showers ahead of it may be possible across 
far northwestern Missouri. Sunday is expected to be dry and warm as 
the cold front stall north and west of the forecast area with highs 
remaining in the 70s. Sunday into Sunday night a upper level trough 
over the Four Corners region will weaken and move into the central 
Plains. This feature will finally drive the cold front into the area 
on Monday bringing the chance for showers (15-30%) however, moisture 
will be meager. Highs Monday will be in the mid 60s to lower 70s. 
Surface high pressure will build into the area behind the front on 
Tuesday cooling temperatures nearer to seasonal normals in the 60s.
