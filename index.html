.DISCUSSION...
Issued at 221 PM CDT Mon Oct 26 2026

Water vapor imagery with 500mb heights plotted shows the upper 
ridge axis shifting east and weakening as it does, and a 
shortwave trough lifting northeastward out of Mexico and 
southwestern Texas. As that shortwave moves into Oklahoma and 
Kansas tonight into tomorrow, forcing associated with it will 
lead to isolated to scattered showers, and possibly a few 
thunderstorms, across eastern/ northeastern Kansas and 
northwestern Missouri. Instability is very weak to nonexistent 
across the area so the chances for any storms is quite low. So 
the general expectation is for be just showers. This system will
also lead to a brief cool down (though still above normal) in 
temperatures Tuesday. 

Temperatures rebound Wednesday through Friday as persistent 
southerly flow allows temperatures to climb back into the upper 70s 
to potentially near 80 degrees. With normal highs in the lower 60s 
for the Kansas City area, we should be 15 to as much as 20 degrees 
above normal. There is some uncertainty regarding this, however. A 
frontal boundary may slide south through the area leading to 
increased precipitation chances and cooler (though still above 
normal) temperatures. For now, the most probable scenario is for 
precipitation to occur across northern Missouri, in the vicinity of 
the boundary, with areas south of that boundary to climb one again 
into the upper 70s to near 80 degrees. A similar scenario plays out 
Friday, with the most likely areas to see precipitation across 
northern Missouri/ northeastern Kansas, and south of that climb into 
the upper 70s. If precipitation occurs further south, that would 
limit just how warm much of the forecast area gets. That's a 
possibility (~<25% of occurrence), just not as probable as what's 
already been mentioned. For Saturday, the best chances for 
precipitation look earlier than in previous forecasts as the 
shortwave moves over the area earlier. This results in decreasing 
PoPs by the early evening hours across eastern Kansas and western 
Missouri. While temperatures trend cooler for the weekend, we may 
still be 10 degrees above normal Saturday - Monday.
